## 1. Core Platforms & Banking Software
*Full-featured platforms designed for mutual credit, microfinance, and local exchange systems.*

* **[Cyclos](https://github.com/sdk2035/cyclos)**
  * **Description:** Open-source online banking system tailored for microfinance institutions, local currencies, time banks, and LETS (Local Exchange Trading Systems). Includes e-commerce and directory features.
  * **Integration Target:** Core backend ledger, user management, and transactional engine for local credit networks.

---

## 2. P2P & Trading Frameworks
*Software and applications focused on facilitating direct exchange and peer-to-peer trade.*

* **[Online Barter Trader](https://github.com/sdk2035/Online-Barter-Trader)**
  * **Description:** Application framework aimed at digitizing and streamlining community-level barter trade.
  * **Integration Target:** Matchmaking engine and user interface for peer-to-peer asset and service exchange.

---

## 3. Modern Tech Stacks & Protocol Libraries
*High-performance, memory-safe implementations and modern backend tools.*

* **[barter-rs](https://github.com/sdk2035/barter-rs)**
  * **Description:** High-performance, modular Rust framework designed for building robust financial and trading systems.
  * **Integration Target:** Core execution engine, high-frequency trading abstractions, or secure data pipelines within the barter ecosystem.

---

## 4. Sector-Specific Integration (Agri-Food & Supply Chains)
*Systems linking local trade to physical supply chains, agriculture, and regional resources.*

* **[Agronet](https://github.com/sdk2035/Agronet)**
  * **Description:** Platform framework focused on agricultural networks, resource tracking, and rural market connectivity.
  * **Integration Target:** Supply chain tracking layer to connect local food production directly with barter markets and regional economic networks.

---

## 5. Conceptual Frameworks & Case Studies
*Articles, governance models, and foundational literature.*

* **[Open Source Barter Currency System (Opensource.com)](https://opensource.com/article/18/9/barter-currency-system)**
  * **Description:** Case study and overview on leveraging open-source tools to build resilient community-based alternative currency and barter systems.
  * **Integration Target:** Conceptual blueprint for community adoption, legal compliance frameworks, and governance design.
